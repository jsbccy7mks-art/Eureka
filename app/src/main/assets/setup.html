Zapp

Set up your TV

Typing on your phone is much quicker than the remote. Whatever you add here appears on your TV straight away.

Xtream login

The server address, username and password from your provider.

TS HLS (M3U8)
Leave on TS unless channels don't play.

M3U link

A playlist link from your provider.

Leave empty to use your provider's own guide.

Stremio add-on

Paste the add-on's install link. It ends in manifest.json, or starts with stremio://.

Stremio account

Brings your Stremio library and Continue watching to your TV.

Used once to sign in. Zapp keeps only Stremio's sign-in key (encrypted), never your password.

Simkl app ID

Create an app at simkl.com/settings/developer (device or TV type) and paste its Client ID. You only do this once; then connect Simkl on the TV.

TMDB

Adds cast photos, age ratings, reviews, "More with…" and "People also watched" to details pages. Get a free key at themoviedb.org/settings/api (the API key or the read access token both work).

Debrid service

Premiumize and TorBox use an API key from your account page. Real-Debrid and AllDebrid sign in with a code on the TV instead.
